Body Types (Somatotypes)

Ectomorph

  • Lean and narrow build
  • Fast metabolism
  • Difficulty gaining weight
  • Low body fat

Mesomorph

  • Naturally muscular
  • Athletic build
  • Balanced metabolism
  • Builds muscle easily

Endomorph

  • Broader build
  • Slower metabolism
  • Gains weight easily
  • Round, soft physique
